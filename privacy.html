Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-19.

What we collect

Only what you type into the interest form: your email address, and optionally your name, your reason for interest and a short note. We also store a one-way, daily-rotating hash of your connection's IP address so we can block spam. We don't use advertising trackers or sell your information.

How we use it

To send occasional updates about Koinonia Kitchen and to understand what people want from it. Your details are stored in a secured database (Supabase) that only the site owner can read.

Deleting your data

To have your details removed, use the form on the home page and write "please delete my data" in the note box with the same email address, and we'll remove every entry for that email. You can also unsubscribe from any update email.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Shared meals, shared life.

Koinonia is the Greek word for fellowship: life shared in common. Koinonia Kitchen is a coming-soon community kitchen and meal ministry idea: cook together, eat together, and make sure no one eats alone. We're gathering volunteers, hosts and neighbors who'd like to take part.

About Koinonia Kitchen

1

Community meals

Free or pay-what-you-can shared meals, open to everyone, served at a long table with neighbors.

2

Cook and serve

Volunteers help plan, cook, serve and clean up. No experience needed, just a willing heart.

3

Partner churches and groups

Congregations and community groups with a kitchen can host a meal night. Tell us about your space.

4

Just getting started

There's no set location or schedule yet. People on the list will hear first when the first meal is planned.
